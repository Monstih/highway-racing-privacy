Privacy Policy for Highway Racing

Effective date: August 7, 2026
Developer: Halo Games
App: Highway Racing (Android package com.halogames.highwayracing)

This policy explains how Highway Racing and its service providers handle information when you use the app.

Information handled

Highway Racing does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, payment information, photos, videos, audio, or files. The app and its advertising and consent providers may automatically process:

  • device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID when available;
  • IP address, network information, and approximate location inferred from them;
  • ad views, clicks, app interactions, and advertising measurement data;
  • device model, operating system, app version, crash, diagnostic, and performance data;
  • consent and privacy choices.

Purposes

This information may be used to provide and measure advertising, perform analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain SDK and app functionality, diagnose problems, and meet legal obligations. Where required, consent or opt-out choices are presented before or during advertising processing.

Advertising and consent providers

The app uses Appodeal mediation with BidMachine, Digital Turbine Exchange, Unity Ads, and Appodeal's consent tooling. Google User Messaging Platform may be included transitively as part of that consent tooling. These providers process information under their own policies:

  • Appodeal Privacy Policy
  • BidMachine Privacy Policy
  • Digital Turbine Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

The current authorized advertising sellers are published at https://monstih.github.io/app-ads.txt.

Your privacy choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. Use the in-app Privacy Options entry when it is available, and use Android settings to reset or limit the Advertising ID.

Send privacy and deletion requests to halogameskz@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Retention, security, and transfers

Information is retained only as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Providers apply their own retention schedules. Data may be processed in other countries and is protected using provider and platform safeguards, including encryption in transit where supported.

Children

This release is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The advertising SDK is configured as not child-directed. Contact us if you believe a child submitted personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date remain available at this URL.

Contact

Privacy and support: halogameskz@gmail.com.
