Privacy Policy for Highway Racing

Effective date: July 30, 2026
Developer: Monstih / Halo Games
App: Highway Racing (Android package com.halogames.highwayracing)

This policy explains how Highway Racing handles information when you use the app.

Information we handle

Highway Racing does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number. The app and its service providers may automatically process:

  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad views, clicks, and other app or ad interactions;
  • device, operating-system, app-version, diagnostic, crash, and performance data;
  • consent and privacy choices.

Why information is used

Information is used to provide and improve the app, display and measure advertising, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose technical problems, and comply with legal obligations. Where required, personalized advertising is used only after valid consent. Otherwise, ads may be non-personalized or limited.

Advertising and consent providers

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy choices. Google may process data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • How AdMob uses app data

Information may be shared with Google and advertising partners only for the purposes described above and subject to applicable consent requirements. Current advertising partners are disclosed through the consent form shown in the app where required.

Your privacy choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising. Use the app's Privacy Options entry to review or change available choices. You may also reset or limit the advertising ID in Android settings.

To submit a privacy request, use the privacy contact form. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public request; ask for a private contact channel if needed.

Data retention and security

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Third-party providers apply their own retention periods. We use reasonable safeguards, but no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

International transfers

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate legal safeguards are used for international transfers.

Children

Highway Racing is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain available at this URL.

Contact
