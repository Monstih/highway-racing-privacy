Privacy Policy for Highway Racing

Version date: November 22, 2026 (documents version 1)
Operator: Halo Games
App: Highway Racing (Android package com.halogames.highwayracing)
Related document: Terms of Use

1. General

1.1. This Policy explains what data is processed when you use the game Highway Racing (the "Game"), why, who receives it and how you can control it. It is written with regard to Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

1.2. The operator of the data processed by the Game itself is Halo Games ("we"). Contact: halogameskz@gmail.com.

1.3. On first launch the Game asks you to confirm that you accept the Terms of Use and this Policy and to tap "Accept". The Game does not start without it. If you do not agree with this Policy, do not use the Game.

1.4. The Game has no account registration of its own: we do not ask for your name, phone number, email, identity documents or payment details. You sign in with your Google Play Games account.

1.5. This Policy is published in Russian and English. If the texts differ, the Russian version prevails.

2. Data we process

2.1. Google Play Games

  • your Google Play Games player ID. The Game uses it only during a session and stores a SHA-256 hash of it instead of the ID itself, so cloud progress never goes to another account;
  • achievements you unlock and your leaderboard results;
  • Play Games statistics events: finished runs (score, distance, overtakes, time at high speed and in the oncoming lane, race place) and your garage (number of cars and their levels);
  • the cloud save (Saved Games) with your Game progress: currency balances, cars and tuning, quests, achievements, daily rewards, nickname, avatar, playtime and the record of your acceptance of the documents. The save is stored in your Google account.

Your Play Games profile name and avatar are shown by Google in leaderboards; we do not store them.

2.2. Google Play purchases

Product ID, purchase token, order number, purchase time and status — to deliver what you bought, restore unfinished purchases and avoid double delivery. Card and other payment details are processed by Google Play; we never receive them.

2.3. Advertising

The Game shows ads through the Appodeal mediation platform and the ad networks connected to it (currently AppLovin and BidMachine; the list may change). The ad SDK starts only after you accept the documents and may process: the Android advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device model, OS version, language, network information, ad views and clicks, and your choices in the ad consent form. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), personalized ads are shown only with your consent, collected by the Appodeal consent form.

2.4. Game settings download

On launch the Game downloads game settings (prices, rewards, texts) from Cloudflare servers. Like any web server, Cloudflare receives your IP address and technical request information.

2.5. Data on your device

Your nickname, avatar, graphics, audio and control settings, progress and the record of your acceptance of the documents (version and time) are stored on the device. Your nickname is not shown to other players: race rivals are computer-controlled.

2.6. Support requests

If you write to us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

2.7. Diagnostics

Google Play may provide us with aggregated crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) that do not identify individual players.

The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise location.

4. Who receives data

Data goes only to partners the Game cannot work without. Each processes it under its own policy:

  • Google LLC — Google Play Games, Saved Games, purchases, Google Play ratings, Android vitals: Google Privacy Policy;
  • Appodeal — ad mediation and consent form: Appodeal Privacy Policy;
  • AppLovin: AppLovin Privacy Policy; BidMachine: BidMachine Privacy Policy;
  • Cloudflare — delivery of game settings: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Authorized ad sellers are listed in app-ads.txt. We also disclose data to public authorities when the law requires it.

5. International transfers

These partners' servers are located outside the Russian Federation, including in the USA and the European Union. By accepting this Policy you agree to the transfer of the data in section 2 to these partners for the purposes in section 3. The partners apply their own safeguards, including encryption in transit.

6. Retention

  • cloud save — in your Google account until you or Google delete it;
  • data on your device — until you delete the Game or its data;
  • purchase records — as long as tax and accounting law requires;
  • support correspondence — up to three years after your last request;
  • advertising data — according to the ad partners' retention periods (see their policies).

7. Your rights

You may ask for information about the processing of your data, request its correction, blocking or deletion, withdraw your consent, and complain to the Russian data protection authority (Roskomnadzor) or a court; depending on where you live, you may have similar rights under local law. Write to halogameskz@gmail.com. We answer within 10 working days, extendable by up to 5 working days with an explanation. To help us find your data, include your nickname and when you last played; never send passwords, card or ID details.

8. Deleting data and controlling ads

  • Settings → Game → "Delete progress" deletes your progress on the device and the cloud save in your Google account. Deleted items, including purchased ones, cannot be restored.
  • You can delete the Game's data in Google Play Games in the Google Play Games app settings.
  • Settings → Game → "Manage ad consent" (shown where applicable) changes your choices in the Appodeal form.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).
  • Withdrawing your consent to this Policy means you stop using the Game: delete the Game and its data and, if needed, write to us.

9. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at younger children. The ad SDK is configured as not child-directed. Users under 14 may play only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, and minors make purchases with their parents' consent. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over secure connections (HTTPS). The cloud save is bound to a hash of your player ID and checked for integrity. We collect only the data the Game needs.

11. Changes

A new version is published at this address with its version date. For material changes the Game will ask you to accept the documents again on launch.
